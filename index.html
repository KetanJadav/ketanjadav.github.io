Research

Charge asymmetry in top + charm production

University of Oklahoma, with Prof. Phillip GutierrezJan 2025 to present

I am measuring the charge asymmetry in events where a top quark is produced together with a charm quark. The asymmetry is a probe of the off-diagonal elements of the CKM matrix and of the structure of the proton, and a place where new physics could show up.

So far I have generated and analyzed simulated events at leading and next-to-leading order, studying transverse momentum, pseudorapidity and missing transverse energy. Next I will apply machine-learning jet tagging and background filtering to look for the asymmetry in LHC data.

Tools MadGraph, Sherpa, Powheg, ROOT, C++, Python

ATLAS Inner Tracker upgrade: testing high- and low-voltage flex circuits

University of OklahomaMay 2025 to present

I work on quality assurance for the flexible circuits that carry high and low voltage in the upgraded ATLAS Inner Tracker (ITk). I run electrical tests to find flexes with abnormal resistance, which is linked to variations in copper-layer thickness that affect detector performance.

I also apply machine learning to automated visual inspection and defect detection in the flexes. The work connects detector hardware with the physics analysis it makes possible.

Tools Electrical testing, data acquisition systems, cleanroom procedures, machine-learning visual inspection

Two-loop Vilkovisky-DeWitt effective action for a scalar field coupled to gravity

Master's thesis, IISER Bhopal, with Prof. Sukanta PandaJul 2022 to Apr 2023

I studied quantum field theory in curved spacetime as a low-energy limit of quantum gravity, relevant to the physics of the early universe. I extended the Vilkovisky-DeWitt covariant effective action to two loops for a minimally coupled scalar field, derived its divergent part, and carried out mass renormalization while keeping the off-shell effective action covariant and gauge invariant.
